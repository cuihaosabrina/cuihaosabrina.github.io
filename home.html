About me

I am a Research Fellow at Trinity College Dublin, where I study human–machine social systems. I am the lead author of a review on AI-enhanced collective intelligence, bringing together complexity science and network science to understand how hybrid systems think, adapt, and make decisions.

My current research focuses on human–AI interaction in collaborative settings: how people perceive and behave toward AI as its roles and levels of agency vary. Through online behavioral experiments, I examine these dynamics and the wider implications of integrating AI into social systems and decision-making.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at University College Dublin’s School of Sociology and Geary Institute for Public Policy. I earned my Ph.D. in Network Science at Central European University in Vienna, studying collective attention, online behavior, and the emergence of popular hashtags through large-scale data analysis.

My research has been featured in The Conversation, de Volkskrant, and MIT Technology Review (Chinese edition).
